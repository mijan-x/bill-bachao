# Air conditioners, fans and the summer peak

Source notes written by Team JU_Segfault. Figures are from the cited reports.

## Why cooling drives the evening peak
Bangladesh's electricity demand roughly doubles from winter to summer. In February 2025 the power adviser said winter demand is around 9,000 MW but summer demand rises to 17,000-18,000 MW (banglanews24). IEEFA reported that on 27 August 2026 demand peaked at 19,027 MW at 9 PM, and that household fan use peaks around 9 PM.

## How much power air conditioners use
Government officials estimated that air conditioners account for about 5,000-6,000 MW of summer demand, and said setting thermostats to 25 C could save 2,000-3,000 MW (banglanews24, February 2025). Another statement put AC load at 4,000-5,000 MW with about 2,000 MW of possible savings (The Business Standard).

## The 25 C advice
The Cabinet Division asked government and private offices and households to keep air conditioners at 25 C or higher (Dhaka Tribune). It is a voluntary appeal: nobody measures or rewards who follows it. Bill Bachao turns it into a measured, optional challenge.

## How much one degree saves
A Daily Star opinion piece reported that raising the AC thermostat by 1 C can save up to about 6 percent of AC energy. Bill Bachao uses a cautious range of 3 to 6 percent per degree and always shows savings as a range, not one exact number.

## Efficient fans
IEEFA estimated that household fans add up to 5,212 MW to summer demand, that energy-efficient BLDC fans are less than 2 percent of household fans, and that replacing old fans with BLDC models could cut summer peak demand by up to 2,590 MW (IEEFA, November 2026).

## Inverter air conditioners
Inverter ACs adjust compressor speed instead of switching fully on and off, so they usually use less electricity for the same cooling. Bill Bachao's upgrade advisor compares nameplate watts and the user's tariff to estimate payback months.
