# Bill Bachao app FAQ

Written by Team JU_Segfault.

## How do I read my prepaid meter?
Most prepaid meters cycle through several screens. Photograph the screen that shows the remaining balance in taka or the total kWh used. Hold the phone straight, avoid glare, and make sure all digits are sharp. Bill Bachao reads the digits; if it is not sure, it asks you to confirm.

## What is the home twin?
The home twin is a small model of your home's electricity use. At first it uses your appliances' nameplate watts and hours. After seven or more meter readings it learns how much your use rises on hot days, so it can split your bill into base load and cooling.

## What is Slab Guard?
Slab Guard projects how many units you will use by the end of the month and warns you before you cross into a more expensive tariff block, especially the jump above 400 units. It also shows the smallest change, such as raising the AC by one or two degrees, that keeps you under.

## Why does my bill go up so much in summer?
In summer, fans and air conditioners run for many more hours. More units push you into higher tariff blocks, so the extra units cost more than the first ones. Above 400 units each extra unit costs Tk 15.01 instead of Tk 9.62.

## Is my data safe?
You choose what to share for each purpose: photos, AI chat, peak challenges and offers. Each household sees only its own data. Partners such as electricity distributors see only area totals.

## বিল বাঁচাও কী?
বিল বাঁচাও মিটারের একটি ছবি থেকে আপনার বিদ্যুৎ বিল কমাতে সাহায্য করে। এটি মাসের শেষে কত ইউনিট হবে তা অনুমান করে এবং বেশি দামের স্ল্যাবে যাওয়ার আগে সতর্ক করে।

## ৪০০ ইউনিটের পরে দাম কত?
জুন ২০২৬ থেকে ৪০০ ইউনিটের পরের প্রতিটি ইউনিটের দাম ১৫.০১ টাকা, আর ৩০১ থেকে ৪০০ ইউনিট পর্যন্ত ৯.৬২ টাকা।
