# Heat safety and fairness rules in Bill Bachao

Rules written by Team JU_Segfault. These are product rules, not medical advice.

## Safety comes before saving
Bill Bachao never asks anyone to set an AC above 27 C and never asks anyone to switch fans off. Comfort and health come first; challenges are always optional and saying no costs nothing.

## Homes with vulnerable members
If a household marks an elderly person, an infant, a pregnant woman or someone who is ill, its AC limit is 26 C. On nights when the heat index is above 41 C, that household receives no challenge at all, only safety tips.

## Lifeline households
Households using 50 units or less a month are lifeline consumers. They receive saving tips but never peak challenges, so the poorest homes do not carry the grid's problem.

## Signs of heat illness
Dizziness, confusion, very high body temperature, or stopping sweating in strong heat can be signs of heat illness. Bill Bachao does not give medical advice: in an emergency in Bangladesh call 999 or go to the nearest health centre. Drink water, stay in shade, and keep fans running.

## Privacy rules
Bill photos are cleaned of names, addresses and account numbers on the team's own computer before any cloud model sees them. The app does not collect GPS or contacts; the area is stored at thana level. Photos are deleted after 30 days.
