Read my meter · মিটার পড়ুন Live

Take or upload a photo of your electricity meter's display. Gemini vision reads the digits into strict JSON; if a digit is not clear it returns nothing rather than a guess, and low-confidence readings ask you to check.

Privacy: photograph only the meter display — not your bill, name or account number. The photo goes to Google's Gemini model to read the digits and is not stored by Bill Bachao; we keep only a usage count for daily limits (20 photos per person per day).

BERC residential (LT-A) tariff from the June 2026 billing cycle. Progressive blocks; lifeline rate only when total use ≤ 50 units; 5% VAT. Check the demand charge on your own bill.

Peak Hour Hero · পিক আওয়ার হিরো Preview for the final

On hot evenings, households get a voluntary challenge for the 8–10 PM peak. The safety rules run first. Savings are shown as a range and verified later with before/after meter photos.

Ask the coach · কোচকে জিজ্ঞাসা করুন Live

Questions go to our live coach on Supabase. It searches a source-cited knowledge base (BERC tariff, cooling, the evening peak, safety rules, app FAQ) with hybrid search: meaning (vector) + keywords + a small knowledge graph. Ask in Bangla or English.

The coach never calculates a bill (the tariff calculator does) and follows the safety rules: no AC above 27°C, never fans off, no medical advice. Written answers switch on when the AI key is added; until then it shows the best-matching sources, which are always shown so you can check them.
